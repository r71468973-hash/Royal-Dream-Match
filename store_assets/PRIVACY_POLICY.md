# Privacy Policy for Royal Dream Match

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Application Name:** Royal Dream Match  
**Package Name:** `com.royaldream.match`  
**Contact Email:** support@royaldreammatch.com  

---

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how **Royal Dream Match** ("we", "our", or "the app") handles player information. We value your privacy and are committed to ensuring clear, transparent data practices compliant with Google Play Policies.

## 2. Google AdMob Advertisements (Banner, Interstitial & Rewarded)
The app uses **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)** to serve non-intrusive banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements shown strictly at natural breaks (such as after a completed level), and opt-in rewarded video advertisements (for players to optionally watch videos for extra coins, lives, or moves). Advertisements are never displayed during active puzzle gameplay. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and diagnostic crash logs in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Information Collected & Used
- **Personal Information:** We do NOT collect, transmit, or sell any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.
- **Local Gameplay Data:** All game progress (including completed levels 1–100, star ratings, coin balances, booster inventories, and sound/music toggles) is stored locally on your device storage (via LocalStorage/SharedPreferences). This data never leaves your device unless backed up by your standard Android OS backup.
- **Network Requests:** Used solely for Google AdMob banner ad delivery and Live Over-The-Air (OTA) game level updates.

## 4. Permissions Used
The app requests only standard Android network permissions:
- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Used for AdMob ad requests and Live OTA game content updates.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Used to check whether the device is connected to the internet before fetching ad units.

The app does **NOT** request runtime dangerous permissions (e.g. Location, Contacts, Camera, Microphone, or Storage access).

## 5. Google Play Families & Children's Privacy
Royal Dream Match is designed for players of all ages. We comply with the Google Play Families Policy. AdMob requests are configured with family-friendly ad ratings.

## 6. User Data Control & Deletion
You can reset or erase all your saved game progress at any time by:
1. Opening the in-game Settings menu (`⚙️`) and tapping **"Reset Game Progress"**.
2. Or clearing the app data in Android Settings > Apps > Royal Dream Match > Storage > Clear Data.
