👑 Royal Dream Match – Privacy Policy

Google AdMob Integrated • Google Play Compliant

Last updated: November 2026 | Effective immediately

1. Overview

Royal Dream Match (Package: com.royaldream.match) is developed as a casual mobile match-3 puzzle game. This Privacy Policy informs players regarding our policies on the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information.

2. Google AdMob Advertisements (Banner, Interstitial & Rewarded)

Royal Dream Match integrates with Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve non-intrusive banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements shown strictly at natural breaks (such as after a completed level), and opt-in rewarded video advertisements (for earning in-game extra coins or lives). Advertisements are never displayed during active puzzle gameplay. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, and general diagnostic telemetry in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Information Collection and Storage

Royal Dream Match does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number.

  • Gameplay Data: Level progression (levels 1–100), stars earned, coins balance, booster inventories, and sound/music settings are stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage and Android App Data. This data never leaves your device.
  • Network Access: Used for Google AdMob banner requests and Live Over-The-Air (OTA) game level content updates.

4. Google Play Families & Children's Privacy

Royal Dream Match is designed for all audiences and casual gamers of all ages. All advertisements served adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines and Family-friendly content standards.

5. Permissions Used

Royal Dream Match only requests standard Android network permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for Google AdMob banner requests and Live OTA updates.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify if a valid internet connection is available before requesting ad units.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage permissions.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all gameplay progress is kept locally on your device, you have full control over your data. You may reset your progress at any time via the in-game Settings menu ("Reset Game Progress") or by clearing the app data in Android Settings > Apps > Royal Dream Match > Storage > Clear Data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We advise you to review this page periodically for any updates.
